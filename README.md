# NLP Truth Classifier 
An NLP Truth Classifier (or Fake News / Deception Detector) is a Natural Language Processing and Machine Learning system built to evaluate textual content and classify it as genuine (truth/authentic) or deceptive (fake/misleading).In modern data science workflows, these pipelines process unstructured text or metadata, transform it into numerical features, and evaluate prediction accuracy using classification algorithms.

Core Pipeline Architecture[Raw Input Text] ──> [Preprocessing & Tokenization] ──> [Vectorization / Embeddings] ──> [ML / DL Classifier] ──> [Truth vs. Fake Output]
1. Data Preprocessing & CleaningText Normalization: Converts text to lowercase, strips URLs, HTML tags, punctuation, and special characters.
2. Stop Word Removal & Tokenization: Filters out non-informative words (e.g., "the", "is") and splits sentences into individual token vectors.
3. Lemmatization/Stemming: Reduces words to their root forms (e.g., "claiming" $\rightarrow$ "claim").
4. a). Feature Extraction (Text Representation)TF-IDF (Term Frequency-Inverse Document Frequency): Measures how unique or important a word is within a document relative to the dataset.Bag of Words (BoW) & N-Grams: Captures word frequencies and short contextual phrases.Word Embeddings & Transformers: Uses contextual vectors (e.g., Word2Vec, GloVe, or BERT embeddings) to represent complex semantic relationships.
5. b) Machine Learning & Deep Learning ModelsTraditional Classifiers: Logistic Regression, Random Forests, Naïve Bayes, and Support Vector Machines (SVM) provide baseline accuracy and fast execution.Deep Learning & Transformers: LSTMs, Bi-RNNs, or fine-tuned Transformer models (like BERT) analyze sequence dependencies and nuanced linguistic patterns.Key ApplicationsFake News & Misinformation Detection: Scans news articles, posts, or headlines to flag unverified claims.Clickbait & Sentiment Analysis: Identifies emotionally charged, manipulative, or deceptive headlines.Review & Feedback Verification: Detects computer-generated or incentivized fake product reviews on e-commerce platforms.Financial & Loan Statement Analysis: Flags fraudulent claims or inconsistent narrative entries in loan application forms.
